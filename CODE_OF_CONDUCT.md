# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. We welcome people of every background and skill level.

* Be respectful. No harassment, insults or discrimination.
* Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.
* Keep discussion on the project. Do not share or request copyrighted game files.
* Maintainers may remove comments, close issues and block people who break these rules.

Report concerns privately through the repository's *Security* or contact options.
