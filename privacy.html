Privacy

Short version: there are no accounts, no tracking and no ads. Your games and saves stay on your device.

What is kept on your device

  • Your games in My Collection, and save states, save files and screenshots, are stored in your browser (IndexedDB and local storage).
  • Settings such as picture options, key mappings, favourites, play time and notes are stored in your browser.
  • A copy of the site (pages, scripts and the emulator cores you have used) is cached by the service worker so the app works offline.

Clearing the site's data in your browser removes all of it. Use Export options in the F1 panel first if you want a backup.

What is sent over the network

  • The site's own files are loaded from the server that hosts it. Game files and saves are never uploaded.
  • The Get the app page asks GitHub (api.github.com) for the list of release files. GitHub sees your address, as with any web request.
  • Report a problem opens a GitHub issue form with your browser details filled in. Nothing is sent until you press Submit there.
  • Netplay, if you turn it on, connects to the server you choose.

What is not collected

No cookies, analytics, advertising identifiers, fingerprinting, or account data. The desktop and Android apps include the same files and make no extra connections.

Your games

Only play games you have the right to use: homebrew, public domain, licensed to you, or backups of games you own where your local law allows it. The site ships no commercial games.
